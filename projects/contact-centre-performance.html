Analytics Case Study

Contact Centre
Performance

A portfolio case study exploring how contact centre performance data can be used to understand service levels, queue pressure, staffing impacts, and operational trends.

Business Problem

Contact centres need to balance customer wait times, service levels, staffing, occupancy, and workload across changing demand patterns. This project investigates where performance pressure appears and how reporting can support better operational decisions.

Questions Asked

  • Which time periods show the highest queue pressure?
  • What patterns appear across SLA, occupancy, and abandoned contacts?
  • Where could staffing or forecasting improvements reduce operational pressure?

  • Occupancy
