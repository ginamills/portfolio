Customer Experience
Dashboard

85,907 Support Interactions • Power BI • Customer Analytics • Data Visualisation

A Power BI dashboard analysing customer satisfaction, support volume, sentiment, issue types, product categories, and support channel performance to identify customer experience trends and operational improvement opportunities.

Business Problem

Customer support leaders need clear visibility of which channels, issue types, and product categories are driving support demand and customer satisfaction. This dashboard was designed to turn customer interaction data into an executive view of performance, sentiment, and improvement opportunities.

Dashboard Preview

Questions Asked

  • Which support channels achieve the strongest customer satisfaction?
  • Which issue types generate the highest support volume?
  • How does customer satisfaction vary across product categories?
  • What proportion of customer interactions result in positive sentiment?
  • Where are there data quality gaps that could affect customer insight reporting?

Key Metrics

  • 85,907 total support interactions analysed
  • Average CSAT score: 4.24 out of 5
  • 1,371 support agents across 3 support channels
  • 40 unique supervisors
  • Sentiment breakdown by positive, negative, and neutral interactions

Key Findings

Outbound support achieved the highest average customer satisfaction score, while email recorded the lowest CSAT result across the support channels.

Returns represented the highest volume issue category while maintaining strong customer satisfaction, demonstrating that high support demand did not necessarily correspond with poor customer experience outcomes.

Data quality review identified two reporting limitations. A significant proportion of support interactions lacked product categorisation, resulting in a large "Unclassified" category. Additionally, the reported support agent count appeared unusually high relative to overall interaction volume, suggesting potential duplication, historical records, or inconsistent source data definitions.

Recommendations

Introduce a mandatory product category field within the customer support workflow to reduce uncategorised records and improve the accuracy of product-level reporting.

Review agent and user identifiers within the source system to determine whether duplicate, inactive, or historical records are inflating the reported support agent count.

Continue monitoring high-volume issue types such as returns and order-related enquiries, while prioritising improvement initiatives for lower-performing categories including product queries and email support interactions.
